# Z Axis

![Z axis](/img/old/2018/10/IMG_20181029_133304.jpg)

:::info Important
Please do not attach anything to this assembly above the tool mount. You will see lots of images of people attaching cable chains, wires, and vacuum hoses to the motor mount. This is the absolute **worst** place on the entire machine to attach anything! It will act as a torque multiplier and completely ruin your accuracy/precision. Anything necessary should be attached to the main gantry assembly there is lots of room on the XYZ piece.
:::

---

![Z axis parts 1](/img/old/2018/10/IMG_20181029_1235462.jpg)
![Z axis parts 2](/img/old/2018/10/IMG_20181029_1238532.jpg)

The tool mount piece uses either #6 lock nuts or M4. Insert the nuts facing the right direction and if they are not a snug fit a tiny drop of glue in the corner will keep them from falling out. I use cyanoacrylate gel. It is not doing anything other making sure they do not fall out when not in use.

---

![Z axis nut traps 1](/img/old/2018/10/IMG_20181029_1219562.jpg)
![Z axis nut traps 2](/img/old/2018/10/IMG_20181029_1225043.jpg)

Insert the 4 nuts into the nut traps, if they aren't a tight fit a drop of glue here will help in assembly later.

---

![Z axis rails 1](/img/old/2018/10/IMG_20181029_124132.jpg)
![Z axis rails 2](/img/old/2018/10/IMG_20181029_1243072.jpg)
![Z axis rails 3](/img/old/2018/10/IMG_20181029_124713.jpg)

Insert the nut traps into your cut and drilled Z rails, pointed end up. Align the holes. Get all four screws 3/4″ (M4 X 20) started but leave them extremely loose for a later alignment step.

---

![Z lower bracket 1](/img/old/2018/10/IMG_20181029_1248182.jpg)
![Z lower bracket 2](/img/old/2018/10/IMG_20181029_124903.jpg)

Press the bearing into the Z-Lower bracket. Make sure it is seated all the way and not crooked.

---

![Z motor mount 1](/img/old/2018/10/IMG_20181029_130244_12.jpg)
![Z motor mount 2](/img/old/2018/10/IMG_20181029_1308082.jpg)
![Coupler use](/img/old/2018/07/Coupler-use.jpg)

Tighten the coupler onto the shaft, first tighten grub screw on the stepper shaft flat spot, then the grub screw on the round part. Make sure the shaft is most of the way into the coupler as shown in the cutaway. Use 3 M3x10mm screws to attach the stepper to the Z-Motor bracket.

Do not insert the Lead Screw yet. The picture is to show you how far the shaft should be set.

---

![Z assembly](/img/old/2018/10/IMG_20181029_131152.jpg)

Use no screws on the Z mounts yet. Slide the Z-lower bracket onto the z rails open bearing side will face away from the tool mount. Slide it all the way to just above the tool mount. Next add the motor mount. This step lets you align the rails and snug up the tool mount 4 screws 3/4″ (M4 X 20). Snug, if you hear a crack you went overboard.

---

![Z lower position 1](/img/old/2018/10/IMG_20181029_1317492.jpg)
![Z lower position 2](/img/old/2018/10/IMG_20181029_1316542.jpg)

Slide the lower tool mount to where it just touches the coupler but does not compress it at all.

:::warning IMPORTANT STEP
Make sure the coupler touches the bearing so it can support the weight of the tool. We are using the ball bearing as a thrust bearing (not the right type of bearing but it is a tiny load).
:::
