# LowRider CNC v4

The LowRider CNC v4 is the V1 Engineering version of a CNC router that can handle up to full sheet
material, or down to a small bench top material removal monster!

![LowRider v4](/img/lowrider/v4/lr4_neon.jpg)

## Key Points

* The average total build cost is about $650 if you buy the full kit from the shop. The only thing
  not included is a ~$120 router and a table. If you print your own parts you can lower that to just
  over $400. Most people get cutting for under $800 USD.
* Foams, wood, and plastic are easy; aluminum and other soft metals are no problem for an experienced
  user. A few users have even machined steel.
* A lot of the parts are 3D printed. To save shipping large parts, the machine can be partially
  assembled to cut them itself.
* Easily removable from the table for storage.
* Inexpensive hardware store conduit is the recommended rail. Rails from 29.5 mm (1" EMT) up to
  32 mm OD work.
* Many tool options — routers, lasers, plasma, pens, etc. Blank DIY mount files are available for
  custom functionality.
* Full Y axis squaring, Z axis leveling, and Z probing are standard for excellent precision.
* Works with any 5-driver board; we have a purpose-built Jackpot CNC Controller with excellent
  performance and usability.
* Can be used with almost any firmware: Marlin, RepRap firmware, GRBL, FluidNC, GRBLHal, or others.
* Want an idea of build time from [actual users?](https://forum.v1e.com/t/lr4-how-many-hours-to-build-lr4/45641/1)

![LowRider v4 Core](/img/lowrider/v4/lr4_core.jpg)

## Size and Shape

* A typical build has a working volume of full sheet, 1/2 sheet, or quarter sheet. In the US:
  8'×4', 4'×4', 4'×2'.
* A standard build gives 50 mm of Z cutting depth; a drop table provides all 100 mm.
* The CNC can technically handle even larger sizes, but smaller builds cut faster.
* The single Y rail keeps the machine properly constrained while maintaining ease of use.
* Width (X axis, "Beam") should always be the shorter axis.
* This machine is most rigid when working near the table surface.

## Tool Options

This is a CNC platform, so just about any tool can be mounted. The most typical is a router or
spindle, diode laser, pen, or drag knife. The
[Makita 700](https://amzn.to/3NgdqGg) series is available worldwide and highly recommended.
Here is the current tool mount collection on
[Printables](https://www.printables.com/@V1Engineering/collections/1752399).
