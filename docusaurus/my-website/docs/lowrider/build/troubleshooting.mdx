# Troubleshooting

Having trouble? Check here first, then ask on the [V1 Engineering Forum](https://forum.v1e.com).

## Motion & Mechanics

**The machine hits a hard stop before the endstop triggers.**  
The Y endstop screws are too far in. Adjust them outward so the endstop triggers just before the
machine reaches the physical stop. Target: trigger with about 4 mm of travel remaining.

**The endstop lever is getting popped or bent.**  
The Y stop screw is set too far out (or missing). The endstop should trigger before the machine
reaches its travel limit, not after.

**The lower X rail is touching the metal XZ plate.**  
This causes driving or lifting issues and can prevent the Z axis from moving freely. The lower X
rail must not contact the XZ plate. Verify your rail lengths against the [calculator](./calculator)
values.

**The beam sits crooked / heels and toes don't match.**  
Check back wheel alignment on both YZ plates — outer faces of the plate and wheel mount should be
flush. The lower strut plate screws at each end control the heel-toe distance.

**The Core tightens and loosens as it crosses over braces.**  
The top two Core tension bolts are too tight. Back them off. Loose is always better than too tight
here — it is simple to add tension later.

**Z binding — the leadscrew feels stiff or the Z motor skips.**  
Check your Z stubs: they must be perpendicular to the leadscrew. Any tilt or twist binds the nut.
Also check that the brass nut is properly seated in the Z\_Nut part.

## Belts

**Belt tension — how tight should it be?**  
Target about 7 lbs (3.2 kg) of tension. It will **not** pluck like a guitar string. When in doubt,
go looser. The parts will break long before the belt does.

**Cuts are consistently several millimeters short or long.**  
The belt pitch is slightly off from nominal. Run the [belt calibration](./calibration) procedure to
measure your actual pitch and adjust steps/mm.

**The endstop lever keeps getting hit during homing.**  
The Y stop screw needs adjustment — set it so the endstop triggers about 4 mm before the end of
physical travel.

## Squaring & Leveling

**My cuts aren't square — diagonals don't match.**  
Follow the [squaring procedure](./calibration). If the difference is over 2 mm, adjust the Y
pull-off in firmware. Under 1 mm is very good; under 2 mm is acceptable for most work.

**One side of a surfacing pass is higher than the other.**  
Run [Z leveling](./calibration) to equalize both Z endstop positions. Getting under 0.1 mm
difference between sides is achievable.

## Printing & Parts

**My printed parts have bad bridges or internal features look wrong.**  
Cura-based slicers can behave differently from Repetier/PrusaSlicer. Check the Printables
thumbnails for expected orientation. Verify the "Dust Skirt" and "YZ\_Plate" bridges span the
short way.

**Nuts won't press into Z stubs or Core.**  
Try threading an M5 bolt through the XZ plate and using it to pull the nut into place (for tight
prints). For loose prints, a flat head screwdriver can press them in directly.
