# Printed Parts

A lot of the LowRider v4 is 3D printed. To save shipping large parts, the machine can even be
partially assembled first to cut its own permanent strut plates.

## Buy or Print?

**Buy** — Rather not spend time printing? Buy a set from the
[V1 Shop](https://www.v1e.com/products/lowrider-v4-printed-parts-with-aluminum-xz-plates).
In Germany/EU: [HaWiWe](https://hawiwe.de/produkt/lowrider-4-druckteile).

**Print your own** — Files available at:

* [Printables.com](https://www.printables.com/model/1034840-lowrider-4-cnc)
* [Thingiverse](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6907717)
* [Maker World](https://makerworld.com/en/models/976448-lowrider-4-cnc?from=search#profileId-1438932)

## Print Settings

* No supports; keep the default orientation.
* About 2.7 kg of filament for a full set with tool mount and board box.
* PLA is recommended for ultimate rigidity. PET-GF/CF and PC-GF/CF are good alternatives with heat
  resistance.
* 3 walls, rectilinear or cubic infill. Thicker layers are fine — no more than 80% nozzle-to-layer
  ratio to keep steep walls working.
* 200 mm × 200 mm × 190 mm available build volume needed.

:::info
Printer skew calibration is suggested to ensure parts are true vertical. A carpenter's square and a
tall printed part work well, or use a [Vector3D CaliLantern](https://vector3d.shop/products/calilantern-calibration).
Skew calibration is sufficient; size calibration should not be needed.

You can also print the two small parts, Z\_Stub and Z\_Nut, first to verify fit and practice M5
nut insertion before printing the larger parts.
:::

* "###" = various sizes.
* **Brace note** — Print Brace\_Max\_### and one Brace\_### at 60% infill. These are the end braces and benefit from added rigidity.
* If a part name is followed by a version number, use the highest one. V1, V2, V3… means there was a fix or edit; a bigger number is the newer part.

:::warning
Repetier, Slic3r, Prusa Slicer, Orca, and Bambu all seem to slice normally. Cura-based slicers can
behave differently. Make sure the parts are aligned on your bed as shown in the Printables thumbnails.
Bridging can also be an issue for some internal overhangs — check the "Dust Skirt" and "YZ\_Plate"
internal features after slicing to verify the bridges span the short way. Repetier/PrusaSlicer-based
slicers have been tested to work as expected.
:::
