# Parts & Where to Buy

## Flat Parts

### Strut Plates

Strut plates give the X axis beam its rigidity. Assemble the machine with printed temporary strut
plates first, then use the machine to cut its own permanent plates.

* Rigid material 6.35 mm (1/4") or thinner.
* Common options: MDF, hardboard, plastic. Metal is premium and not necessary.

[Vector76 Strut Generator](https://vector76.github.io/Web_OpenSCAD_Customizer/strut_plate.html) —
Jamie made this easy-to-use site for any size you can dream of. The strut length comes from the
[calculator](./calculator), but it cuts slightly smaller (0.5 mm) by design.

* `strut_length` = value from the [calculator](./calculator)
* `num_braces` = leave at default
* `size of dogbone...` = leave at 3.5 for a 1/8" (3 mm) endmill
* Check both `front_plate` and `bottom_plate`
* `front_wing_size` = 11.5 for 29.5 mm (1" EMT conduit), or your conduit size
* Click `Render (Ctrl+Enter)` → download `strut_plate.svg`

![Strut Plate](/img/lowrider/v4/strut.png)

Extra info:
* The "Wings" are the parts that make the plate nearly touch the rails to fill in the gaps.
* A full sheet build normally has 7 braces.

### XZ Plates

XZ Plates connect the X axis beam to the YZ plates.

The easiest option: metal XZ plates from the [V1E.com shop](https://www.v1e.com/products/lowrider-4-aluminium-xz-plates)
or, in the EU, from [HaWiWe](https://hawiwe.de/produkt/aluminium-xz-platten/).

Other options:
* Cut your own metal plates with the DXF files found with the printed parts. Aluminum or steel
  1/4" (6.35 mm) thick. If using thicker/thinner material, adjust the 16 M3×10 mm screw length.
* [Printable XZ plates](https://www.printables.com/model/1047962-printable-xz-plates-for-lowrider-4-consider-alu-in)
  by member Aza — only recommended if you have aluminum-cutting experience on a V1 CNC.
