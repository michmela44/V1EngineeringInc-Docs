# Core Assembly

Ready to start your build? Relax, have fun. If you have issues, stop and ask in the forums. Taking a
step back and asking a couple of questions can save a ton of headaches. **Hand tools only** — power
tools get you into trouble here.

---

![Core Assembly step a](/img/lowrider/v4/ca.jpg)

* Bearings should drop into the pockets.
* Bolts get inserted in the direction shown.
* Snug just enough to seat the bolt heads — these are just bearing axles, no need to crush anything.

---

![Core Assembly step b](/img/lowrider/v4/cb.jpg)

* Same thing again.
* Bearings should drop into the pockets.
* Bolts get inserted in the direction shown.

---

![Core Assembly step c](/img/lowrider/v4/cc.jpg)

* Third time.

---

![Core Assembly step d](/img/lowrider/v4/cd.jpg)

* Okay, this time it's different.
* Insert the bolts in the direction shown.
* **Do not snug these down!**
* These two bolts control the tension of the Core on the X rails.
* Leave these loose — we will slowly tighten them when the machine is fully assembled.

---

![Core Assembly step e](/img/lowrider/v4/ce.jpg)

* This step uses a piece of filament to lock in the tool holder nuts.

---

![Core Assembly step f](/img/lowrider/v4/cf.jpg)

* Insert the M5 nuts with the nylock side facing the back (the side shown in this picture).
* For each side, cut the filament at a small angle to make it easier to feed into the lower hole.
* Slide the filament in, making sure it seats all the way to the bottom of the second hole.
* Snip the top off flush with the core surface to avoid snags.

---

![Core Assembly step g](/img/lowrider/v4/cg.jpg)

* On this side the angle goes the other way.

---

![Core Assembly step h](/img/lowrider/v4/ch.jpg)

* Repeat for all 4 screws.
* Make sure they are all snipped flush.

---

![Core Assembly step i](/img/lowrider/v4/ci.jpg)

* Now is a good time to add your tool mount.
* This lets you make sure nuts get seated well and nothing pushes out or gets cross-threaded.

---

![Core Assembly step j](/img/lowrider/v4/cj.jpg)

* Snug these up real good, but don't break anything.
* I always get all 4 screws close, then fully tighten the left screws first on both top and bottom
  mounts.
* If you need to tram your tool later, a couple layers of tape as a shim under these mounts will
  usually do the trick.

---

![Core Assembly touch plate](/img/lowrider/v4/ck.jpg)

* Optional: touch plate.
* This can be added later or just ran behind the router; it does not have to use this hole.

---

![Core Assembly touch plate wire](/img/lowrider/v4/cl.jpg)

* Leave at least 140 mm (5.5") of free touch plate wire.
* Run the rest through the hole.

---

![Core Assembly wire tunnel](/img/lowrider/v4/cm.jpg)

* Run that wire through the tunnel in the core.

---

![Core Assembly wire routing](/img/lowrider/v4/co.jpg)

* From here you will also want to run the X endstop wire.
* Take your time, give it a little jiggle — they should slide right through.
* Worst case: guide the black plug through the opening at the front of the core.
* After getting the wires through to the front, label them.

---

![Core Assembly wire crimp](/img/lowrider/v4/cn.jpg)

* For these small endstop wires I like to fold the wire back and crimp them this way.
* Not "proper" form, but for such small gauge wire it works very well.
* Testing these crimps for continuity is a good idea.

---

![Core Assembly endstop plug](/img/lowrider/v4/cp.jpg)

* Plug in the X endstop.
* Uses M2.5 mm screws.

---

![Core Assembly endstop screw](/img/lowrider/v4/cq.jpg)

* Lever opens to the bottom.
* Seat these tiny screws all the way — be very careful not to strip them.

---

![Core Assembly pulley](/img/lowrider/v4/cr.jpg)

* Using the belt guide on the back of the core, set the pulley on the X stepper shaft.
* Always tighten the set/grub screw on the shaft flat first, then the second screw.

---

![Core Assembly stepper](/img/lowrider/v4/cs.jpg)

* M3 screws attach the stepper.
* Inserting the X belt now saves a bit of work later.
* Run all wires together up from behind the stepper.
* Wires should not be constricted.

---

![Core Assembly wire secure](/img/lowrider/v4/ct.jpg)

* Secure the wires up at the top of the core.
* Next step uses idlers and M5 hardware.

---

![Core Assembly idler nuts](/img/lowrider/v4/cu.jpg)

* Insert the M5 nuts.
* A flat-headed screwdriver or needle-nosed pliers can press them in.

---

![Core Assembly idlers](/img/lowrider/v4/cv.jpg)

* Add the idlers and screws.
* Use the holes in the top of the core to drive the screws.
* These just need to hit the Nylock — no need to be snug.
* Idlers should be extremely free to rotate.

---

![Core Assembly complete](/img/lowrider/v4/cx.jpg)

* That's it — the Core is ready for final assembly!
