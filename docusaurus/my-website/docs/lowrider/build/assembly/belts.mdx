# Belt Installation

## Y Rail Layout

![Belt Y rail layout](/img/lowrider/v4/yr1.png)

* First step to mounting the CNC to the table is setting the Y rail.
* Do your best to make a perfectly rectangular table; once the rail is set that becomes your
  reference.
* The belt holders and Y\_Clips all share the outer edge.
* The [calculator](../calculator) "table measurement" gives you the exact outside dimensions of the
  belt holders as the footprint.
* No more than 300 mm gap recommended between Y\_Clips.

---

![Belt Y clip layout](/img/lowrider/v4/yr2.png)

* Pre-drilling is highly recommended so your parts lay flat when screwed down.
* Draw a screw hole centerline down the table to make this easy.
* There is ±1.5 mm so each clip has a little wiggle room.
* Set both Y Max belt ends and the first/last Y clips on the table first.
* Then measure the remaining gap and equally divide the rest of your clips.

---

![Belt Y min screw](/img/lowrider/v4/yr3.jpg)

* Make sure both Y Min belt holders get an M3 screw fully inserted.
* This is for coarse Y squaring adjustment.

---

![Belt hole marking tip](/img/lowrider/v4/yr3a.jpg)

* Trick for marking holes: an over-sharpened pencil.

---

![Belt both ends set](/img/lowrider/v4/yr4.jpg)

* Both ends get set and pre-drilled.

---

![Belt first clip](/img/lowrider/v4/yr4b.jpg)

* The first clip gets attached directly next to the belt holders on both ends.

---

![Belt clips on rail](/img/lowrider/v4/yr5.jpg)

* Put the clips on your rail.
* Snug each clip to the table one at a time.
* Use the table edge or any straight surface (a level works well as a straight edge).

---

## Y Belts

![Belt loop screw](/img/lowrider/v4/yb1.jpg)

* Loop the belt around an M3 screw.

---

![Belt lock](/img/lowrider/v4/yb2.jpg)

* Pull the belt to set the screw and lock the belt.
* The end of the belt does come through with a couple extra teeth showing — this is the same for all belts.

---

![Belt Y min set](/img/lowrider/v4/yb3.jpg)

* Set the Y Min into place — this is a quick release for removing from the table.

---

![Belt Y max tensioner](/img/lowrider/v4/yb4.jpg)

* Prepare the Y\_Max belt tensioner.

---

![Belt tensioner nut](/img/lowrider/v4/yb5.jpg)

* Sink the nut and insert the screw. The Nylock is closest to the thin end.

---

![Belt secure Y max](/img/lowrider/v4/yb6.jpg)

* Secure the belt with an M3 just as before.
* This end sets the initial tension so it can take a few tries to get the distance right.
* Make sure the belt is on the pulley and in both idlers correctly.

---

![Belt tension setting](/img/lowrider/v4/yb7.jpg)

* You want tension to hold the belt taut but not crazy — 7 lbs (3.2 kg) tension, which is not as
  much as you think.
* It will **not** pluck like a guitar.
* Always start loose. If parts consistently come out several millimeters small, you might be too loose.
* If you overtighten you can prevent steppers from turning, and you will certainly break parts.
* The parts will break long before the belt does.

---

![Belt Y min distance](/img/lowrider/v4/yb8.jpg)

* Setting the Y min distance can be done from the [calculator](../calculator), or
* Using a piece of belt on the table to set a one-belt-gap width, making sure your end-to-end brace
  gap is set to the calculated width so nothing changes when you add your strut plate.
* Mark and pre-drill all holes. A pencil sharpened by hand gets into the deep holes.

---

![Belt Y stop screw](/img/lowrider/v4/yb85.jpg)

* Set the Y stop screw so the endstop triggers just before the LowRider hits the end.
* If the endstop gets past the screw there is a good chance you will pop or bend the endstop lever.
* Take your time. I get a trigger and about 4 mm more travel.
* If you have difficulty, the endstop tab can be bent slightly outward to trigger earlier.

---

![Belt Y min tension](/img/lowrider/v4/yb9.jpg)

* Finish this belt just like the max side.
* M3s to secure the belt, set the correct tension.
* Y axis squaring gets set later.

---

## X Belt

![Belt X start](/img/lowrider/v4/xb1.jpg)

* Now the X belt.
* Wrap around an M3 screw.
* You can pinch the belt and hold it in the metal XZ plate as you work it in further.

---

![Belt X push down](/img/lowrider/v4/xb2.jpg)

* Push the belt and screw to the bottom with a screwdriver.

---

![Belt X tensioner](/img/lowrider/v4/xb3.jpg)

* Load the belt into the X Belt tensioner.
* Make sure to get the distance right.
* Move the core back and forth **slowly** to make sure the belt is on the pulley and idlers properly.

---

![Belt X tension](/img/lowrider/v4/xb4.jpg)

* Tension to ~7 lbs (3.2 kg) same as before. Loose is better than breaking things.

---
