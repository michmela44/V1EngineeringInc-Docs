# Beam & Main Assembly

## Beam Assembly

![Beam assembly parts](/img/lowrider/v4/B1.jpg)

* Gather your beam parts.
* If you bought the V1 printed parts set, you will notice the two end braces have a different surface
  pattern (higher infill). If you printed your own, make sure to use heavy infill on the ends.

---

![Beam assembly tensioner](/img/lowrider/v4/beam1.jpg)

* Install and fully seat the X belt tensioner nut.

---

![Beam assembly seat](/img/lowrider/v4/beam2.jpg)

* Use the screw to fully seat if needed, then take the screw back out.

---

![Beam assembly rails](/img/lowrider/v4/beam3.jpg)

* Position the end braces to fit the temp struts, equally space remaining braces, and snap in the rails.
* Rails must not stick out past your braces. Double-check with the [calculator](../calculator) values
  for total brace width.

---

![Beam assembly temp struts](/img/lowrider/v4/beam4.jpg)

* Install the temp strut plates (orientation shown in the next two steps).
* Do not crush the front rail clamps.
* As you tighten the screws you will feel the clamps start to engage — that is all you need.
* Tubes will still be able to rotate a bit — just a tiny bit of tension is all you need.
* The rails carry no sideways loads. Do not overtighten — it only causes issues.

---

![Beam assembly strut orientation](/img/lowrider/v4/B4.jpg)

* Notice the orientation — there is a screw head cutout.

---

![Beam assembly strut other side](/img/lowrider/v4/B5.jpg)

* Only one way this side fits.

---

![Beam assembly bottom struts](/img/lowrider/v4/beam5.jpg)

* Install the bottom temp struts as well.
* Pay attention to the small hole location.

---

## Main Assembly

![Main assembly YZ max](/img/lowrider/v4/ma1.jpg)

* Screw the YZ max assembly to the beam. These 3 screws get snugged down.
* Hold them with the screwdriver and use a wrench on the nuts.
* Snug — don't crush. Easy to leave them light and check again later.

---

![Main assembly side view](/img/lowrider/v4/ma2.jpg)

* Looking good — almost a machine!

---

![Main assembly core](/img/lowrider/v4/ma3.jpg)

* Slide on the core, being careful not to snag any wires.
* Check the tension on the top two core tension bolts.
* Start loose — sometimes you don't need to tension them until after some use.
* You want the core seated and not rattling when you wiggle it. Tiny adjustments if needed.
* If too tight, you will feel the core tighten and loosen as it crosses over braces.
* Loose is better than too tight. It is simple to add tension later, difficult to replace a broken core.

---

![Main assembly YZ min](/img/lowrider/v4/ma4.jpg)

* Now add the YZ min assembly.

---

![Main assembly admire](/img/lowrider/v4/ma5.jpg)

* Easy section — admire your work.

---

![Main assembly lower rail check](/img/lowrider/v4/ma6.jpg)

* Double-check that the lower X rail is not touching either metal XZ plate.
* If it touches, the machine will likely have driving or lifting issues.

---

![Main assembly square check](/img/lowrider/v4/ma7.jpg)

* Do a quick check with a square to see how your sides are angled.
* If you don't have a square, this gets checked in the next section another way.

---

![Main assembly heel toe](/img/lowrider/v4/ma8.jpg)

* Check that your heel-toe measurements are equal.
* Roughly level the beam by turning the Z axis leadscrews.
* Measure across the front of the machine from the side plates (not the wheels).

---

![Main assembly heel toe back](/img/lowrider/v4/ma9.jpg)

* Now measure across the back.
* The two numbers should be nearly identical.
* If not, the machine can wander when not under power.
* Adjustment: check your end braces; if needed, loosen temp struts and straighten the sides.
